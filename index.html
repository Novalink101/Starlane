HELP CENTRE

Frequently asked questions.

Some things you might want to know about SWARM.

SWARM is an aerial and cable logistics concept designed to transport packages through alternative delivery networks.
Eligible packages would be transported using designated drone routes, subject to operational availability, safety requirements and applicable aviation regulations.
Zipline delivery uses a suspended cable system to transport packages between designated stations.
The SWARM website is being designed to support shipment tracking. Live tracking will require connected tracking infrastructure.
This website is currently a demonstration of the planned customer experience. It does not confirm operational delivery availability or arrange real deliveries.
